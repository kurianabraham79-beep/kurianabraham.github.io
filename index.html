One small exercise that shows how I think. It takes about a minute.

Good analytics starts with dependable data. I bring engineering discipline to the whole path, not just the final chart.

Behind every useful report is a pipeline that collects the right data, transforms it consistently and makes quality visible. Before I trust a number, I check what one row in the table actually means.

Margin note

Before I build a report, I ask one question: what does a single row mean? Here a row is a shipment, not an order. So any order sent in two boxes gets its total counted twice.

Checking the grain first takes minutes. Explaining a wrong number in a meeting takes much longer.
